# LOG KYA KAHENGE? — Roleplay Scripts (Draft 2 & Draft 3)

Performance: 26 November · Time: 10–12 min · Budget: Rs 1,000 · Class participates with placards

---

## PART A — MEMBERS KE LIYE OVERVIEW (yeh group mein share karein)

### Draft 2: "DOCTOR YA ENGINEER?" (Middle ground, funny, family-friendly)
**Kahani:** Matric/FSc result ka din. Beti Zoya chef banna chahti hai, lekin ghar walon ko sirf "log" ki parwa hai: phuppo, khandaan ka WhatsApp group, aur mohalla. "Log Ltd. News" har scene ko freeze karke breaking news deta hai. Zoya ke paas aik **rewind remote** hai. Climax mein wohi scene dobara chalta hai, is dafa "apni marzi se".
**Issues:** career pressure, comparison culture, larkiyon ke career ko "shaadi tak ka timepass" samajhna, family WhatsApp groups.
**Mood:** 80% comedy, 20% emotion. Ending positive hai.

### Draft 3: "FUNERAL KA MENU" (Most controversial, dark satire)
**Kahani:** Dada ji ka inteqal ho gaya hai. Ghar mein rishtedaar jama hain, lekin kisi ko dukh nahi. Bahes biryani ki boti, Dadi ke kapron, jaidaad, aur "kitne log aaye" pe ho rahi hai. "Log Ltd. News" janaze ki "live coverage" karta hai. Ending mein Dada ji ki aik chitthi parhi jati hai, aur pata chalta hai ke woh poori zindagi "log kya kahenge" ki zanjeer mein bandhe rahe aur ab chahte the ke unke baad koi na bandhe.
**Issues:** ghum mein bhi dikhawa, widow ke saath sulook, wirasat ke jhagre, betiyon ka haq, khaane pe tabsira.
**Mood:** pehle 8 min hansi, aakhri 3 min khamoshi. Zyada gehra, zyada risky, lekin yaad reh jane wala.

### Class placards (Gohar shuru mein class mein baant dega)
Gohar ke **"LOG! Aap ka kya khayal hai?"** kehne pe class placard utha kar zor se parhegi.
1. **"Khandaan mein naak kat jayegi!"**
2. **"Chaudhry sahab ka beta toh CSS kar raha hai!"**
3. **"Rishta kaun dega?!"**
4. **"Hamare zamane mein toh..."**
5. **"Mohalla kya kahega?!"**
6. **"Aur log? LOG KYA KAHENGE?!"** (finale, sab placards aik saath)

Gohar ko pehle 20 second mein class ko samjhana hai: *"Aaj aap sab 'LOG' hain. Jab main kahoon 'Log, aap ka kya khayal hai?', toh apna placard utha ke poore josh se parhna hai. Teacher ke saamne sharmana nahi, aaj aap pe koi kuch nahi kahega... kyunke aap khud LOG hain!"*

### Stage directions ka code
- **[FREEZE]**: sab apni jagah ruk jayein, statue ki tarah. Sirf Gohar bolega.
- **[UNFREEZE]**: scene wahin se chalega.
- **[REWIND]**: Zoya remote dabaye, sab ulte chalte hue 3 qadam peeche jayein aur munh se "wiiiiuuu" ki awaaz nikalein. Yeh rehearse karein, sab se zyada hansi isi pe aayegi.

# DRAFT 2 — "DOCTOR YA ENGINEER?"

**Kirdar:**
- **Zoya:** Tahreem
- **Abbu (Rashid sahab):** Usman
- **Ammi:** Mehar
- **Phuppo Shamim:** Ahmed Raza
- **Faraz (bhai):** Ahmed Amir
- **Hira (cousin):** Saadia
- **Aleena (researcher):** Khudema
- **Log Ltd. News Anchor:** Gohar

**Setup:** Darmiyan mein 2 kursiyan aur aik table (drawing room). Aik kona "News Studio" (Gohar ka). Aleena ka kona darwaze ke paas.

---

### SCENE 0 — INTRO (1 min)

*(Gohar mic le kar andar bhaagta hai.)*

**GOHAR:** Assalam o Alaikum Pakistan! Aap dekh rahe hain Pakistan ka sab se tez, sab se ghair-zaroori news channel: **LOG LTD. NEWS!** Hamara motto: *"Aap ki zindagi, hamari marzi!"*

*(Placards baant ta hai aur instructions deta hai, upar diya hua dialogue.)*

**GOHAR:** Aaj ki breaking news: Rawalpindi ke aik aam se ghar mein FSc ka result aa gaya hai! Aur saath hi aa rahi hai... *(drama se)* ...PHUPPO! Chaliye live chalte hain!

---

### SCENE 1 — RESULT KA DIN (2.5 min)

*(Ammi aur Abbu table pe. Zoya phone pakre andar aati hai, khush.)*

**ZOYA:** Ammi! Abbu! Result aa gaya! 1020 marks!

**AMMI:** *(seene pe haath)* Allah tera shukar! Ab medical mein admission pakka!

**ZOYA:** Ammi... main medical mein nahi jaana chahti. Mujhe culinary school jaana hai. Main chef banna chahti hoon.

*(Sannata. Abbu ka chai ka cup hawa mein ruk jata hai.)*

**ABBU:** *(aahista)* Kya... banna hai?

**ZOYA:** Chef, Abbu. Khaana banana. Professionally.

**AMMI:** Khaana toh main 25 saal se bana rahi hoon, mujhe kisi ne "chef" nahi kaha!

**GOHAR:** [FREEZE] Breaking news! Aik larki ne doctor banne se inkaar kar diya hai! Mulk ki GDP 2 percent gir gayi hai. Saari aunties ke WhatsApp groups mein emergency meeting bula li gayi hai. LOG! Aap ka kya khayal hai?

**CLASS:** *(Placard 1)* "KHANDAAN MEIN NAAK KAT JAYEGI!"

**GOHAR:** Bilkul sahi! [UNFREEZE]

*(Darwaze pe dastak. Phuppo dakhil hoti hai, purse ghumate hue.)*

**PHUPPO:** Assalam o Alaikum! Main toh bas guzar rahi thi... socha mubarak de doon. *(Mithai ka dabba kholti hai, khaali hai.)* Haye, raste mein khatam ho gayi. Haan toh Zoya, kitne marks aaye? Meri Sobia ke 1050 aaye the, MBBS kar rahi hai, rishte line mein khare hain!

**ZOYA:** 1020, Phuppo.

**PHUPPO:** *(afsos se)* Chalo koi baat nahi... private medical college mein kara do. Bas 30 lakh lagenge. Plot bech do, izzat toh bachegi.

**AMMI:** *(dheere se)* Shamim... yeh chef banna chahti hai.

**PHUPPO:** *(cheekh ke kursi se uthti hai)* CHEF?! Yani BAWARCHI?! Rashid, tumhari beti dhaaba kholegi? Main Chaudhry sahab ki biwi ko kya munh dikhaoongi? Unka beta toh CSS kar raha hai!

**GOHAR:** Log, aap ka kya khayal hai?

**CLASS:** *(Placard 2)* "CHAUDHRY SAHAB KA BETA TOH CSS KAR RAHA HAI!"

**PHUPPO:** Dekha? Saara mulk mere saath hai!

---

### SCENE 2 — FARAZ AUR HIRA (2 min)

*(Faraz andar aata hai, bag kandhe pe, bilkul thaka hua, robot ki tarah chal raha hai.)*

**PHUPPO:** Aa gaya hamara engineer! Masha'Allah! Faraz beta, batao Zoya ko, engineering kitni achi cheez hai.

**FARAZ:** *(bilkul flat awaaz mein)* Bohat achi cheez hai. Main khush hoon. Yeh Abbu ki marzi thi.

**ABBU:** Meri marzi nahi, tumhari marzi thi!

**FARAZ:** *(wohi flat awaaz)* Ji. Meri marzi thi. Jo Abbu ki marzi thi.

*(Class hansegi. Faraz kursi pe baith ke so jata hai.)*

**GOHAR:** [FREEZE] Exclusive report! Pakistan mein har saal hazaron engineers bante hain. Inmein se kitnon ko engineering pasand hai? Hamari team ne survey kiya... *(kaghaz dekhta hai)* ...jawab aaya: "Ammi se pooch ke batata hoon." [UNFREEZE]

*(Hira andar aati hai, haath mein sketchbook, muskura rahi hai.)*

**HIRA:** Assalam o Alaikum sab ko! Zoya, mubarak ho!

**PHUPPO:** *(munh banate hue)* Aa gayi... fine arts wali. Hira, ab bhi tasveerein banati ho? Kaam dhanda kab shuru karogi?

**HIRA:** Phuppo, main design agency mein kaam karti hoon. Aur do international clients hain. Aap ka naya clinic ka logo bhi maine hi banaya tha.

**PHUPPO:** *(ruk ke)* ...Woh logo toh acha hai.

**HIRA:** Shukriya. Aap ne free mein banwaya tha.

*(Class hansegi.)*

**ZOYA:** Dekha Ammi? Hira baji ne apni marzi ki aur woh khush hain!

**AMMI:** Haan, lekin uska rishta ab tak nahi hua!

**GOHAR:** Log, aap ka kya khayal hai?

**CLASS:** *(Placard 3)* "RISHTA KAUN DEGA?!"

**HIRA:** *(class ki taraf mur ke, aaram se)* Rishta zaroor aayega, log. Bas ab main us ghar mein nahi jaungi jahan meri sketchbook ko "timepass" kaha jaye.

---

### SCENE 3 — OUTSIDER (1.5 min)

*(Aleena notebook le kar aahista andar aati hai. Sab chup.)*

**ALEENA:** Excuse me... main aik research kar rahi hoon. "Log" kaun hain? Sab kehte hain "log kya kahenge". Mujhe unka address chahiye. Main unka interview lena chahti hoon.

*(Sab aik doosre ko dekhte hain.)*

**ABBU:** Log... matlab... mohalla.

**AMMI:** Rishtedaar.

**PHUPPO:** *(fakhar se)* Main!

**ALEENA:** *(notebook mein likhti hai)* Toh "log" sirf aik phuppo hain? *(Zoya ki taraf)* Shukriya.

*(Aleena kone mein jaa kar baith jati hai aur notes leti rehti hai.)*

**GOHAR:** [FREEZE] Pehli dafa kisi ne "log" ka address manga hai! Hamari team ne Google Maps pe search kiya. Result: *"Log: har jagah, kahin bhi nahi."* [UNFREEZE]

---

### SCENE 4 — FAISLA (2 min)

**ABBU:** *(khara hota hai, serious)* Bas! Zoya, tum medical ka form bharo. Main ne zindagi bhar bank mein naukri ki, kyunke Abba ne kaha tha "log kya kahenge" agar dukaan kholi. Main nahi chahta tumhe koi taana de.

**ZOYA:** *(dheeme lekin mazboot)* Abbu... aap ne bhi toh dukaan kholni thi?

*(Abbu chup.)*

**ZOYA:** Aur aap khush hain?

*(Lamba khamosh pause. Abbu baith jata hai.)*

**GOHAR:** *(mic neeche karke, pehli dafa serious)* Log, aap ka kya khayal hai?

**CLASS:** *(Placard 4)* "HAMARE ZAMANE MEIN TOH..."

**ZOYA:** *(class ki taraf)* Haan. Aap ke zamane mein. Aur us zamane ne aap ko kya diya?

*(Zoya remote uthati hai.)*

**ZOYA:** Ruko. Aik dafa phir se. Is dafa... apni marzi se.

**[REWIND]** *(sab "wiiiuuu" karte hue ulte chalte hain, Scene 1 ki jagah pe)*

---

### SCENE 5 — REWIND: APNI MARZI SE (1.5 min)

**ZOYA:** Ammi, Abbu, 1020 marks! Aur main chef banna chahti hoon.

**ABBU:** *(lamba saans, phir muskura ke)* Toh... meri biryani kab banaogi?

**AMMI:** Aur pehli dish mera recipe hogi, credit ke saath!

*(Phuppo dakhil hoti hai.)*

**PHUPPO:** CHEF?! Main Chaudhry sahab ki biwi ko kya...

**ABBU:** Shamim. Chaudhry sahab ki biwi ko Zoya ke restaurant ka discount card de dena.

*(Phuppo munh khole khari reh jati hai, phir purse se phone nikalti hai.)*

**PHUPPO:** Acha... discount kitna hai?

**FARAZ:** *(achanak jaag ke, pehli dafa josh se)* Main bhi engineering chhor raha hoon! Mujhe photographer banna hai!

**SAB:** *(aik saath)* FARAZ?!

**FARAZ:** *(foran wapas flat)* ...Ammi se poochna padega.

---

### ENDING (1 min)

*(Sab freeze. Aleena khari hoti hai, center mein aati hai.)*

**ALEENA:** Meri research mukammal ho gayi. *(notebook band karti hai)* "Log" koi aur nahi hain. Woh hum hain. Aap... *(class ki taraf ishara)* ...aur main. Hum doosron ki zindagi ka faisla karte hain aur apni zindagi doosron ke hawale kar dete hain.

**GOHAR:** Log! Aakhri dafa! Aap ka kya khayal hai?

**CLASS:** *(sab placards)* "AUR LOG? LOG KYA KAHENGE?!"

**SAB ACTORS:** *(aik saath, class ki taraf)* **LOG TOH KUCH BHI KAHENGE!**

**ZOYA:** Faisla aap ka hai: apni zindagi jeeni hai, ya logon ki.

*(Sab jhuk kar salaam. KHATAM.)*

# DRAFT 3 — "FUNERAL KA MENU"

**Kirdar:**
- **Zoya (poti):** Tahreem
- **Abbu (Rashid, bara beta):** Usman
- **Ammi (bahu):** Mehar
- **Phuppo Shamim:** Ahmed Raza
- **Dadi (widow):** Saadia
- **Faraz (cousin):** Ahmed Amir
- **Aleena (researcher):** Khudema
- **Log Ltd. News Anchor:** Gohar

**Setup:** Farsh pe aik safed chaadar. Dadi aik kursi pe chup baithi hain, tasbeeh haath mein. Aik table pe khaali degh aur plates. Gohar ka "studio" kone mein.

**Zaroori note:** Mazaak *rishtedaaron ke dikhawe* pe hai, maut ya mazhab pe nahi. Dadi ka kirdar puri izzat se khela jaye. Woh kabhi joke ka nishana nahi banengi.

---

### SCENE 0 — INTRO (1 min)

*(Gohar dheere dheere, jhoota ghumgeen chehra le kar andar aata hai.)*

**GOHAR:** *(sargoshi mein)* Assalam o Alaikum... yeh hai Log Ltd. News. Aaj hum aik ghumgeen mauqe se live hain. Dada ji, Haji Akram sahab, inteqal kar gaye hain. Inna lillahi wa inna ilaihi raji'un. *(achanak tez awaaz mein)* Lekin asal sawal yeh hai: **BIRYANI MEIN BOTI THI YA NAHI?!** Chaliye live chalte hain!

*(Placards baant ta hai, wohi instructions.)*

---

### SCENE 1 — PHUPPO KI AAMAD (2 min)

*(Ammi aur Abbu thake hue, mehmanon ka intezaar. Zoya Dadi ke paas baithi hai.)*

*(Phuppo dakhil hoti hai: safed designer suit, dhoop ka chashma, dramatic andaaz mein rote hue.)*

**PHUPPO:** HAAAYE ABBA JIII! *(rona achanak band, chashma utaar ke kamra dekhti hai)* Rashid, yeh tent kiraaye ka hai? Itna chhota? Chaudhry sahab ke Abba ke janaze pe 3 tent lage the!

**ABBU:** Baji, yeh waqt hai yeh sab baat karne ka?

**PHUPPO:** Yahi toh waqt hai! Log aayenge, dekhenge, baatein banayenge! *(wapas rona)* Haaaye Abba jiii... *(phir normal)* ...aur khaana kis ka hai? Kis caterer ka?

**AMMI:** Ghar mein banwaya hai... daal chawal.

**PHUPPO:** *(seena pakar ke, dil ka daura jaisa)* DAAL CHAWAL?! Haji sahab ke janaze pe DAAL CHAWAL?!

**GOHAR:** [FREEZE] Breaking news! Janaze pe daal chawal! Khandaan ki izzat khatre mein! Saare rishtedaaron ke "condolence" WhatsApp group mein menu ki tasveer pehle hi forward ho chuki hai! LOG! Aap ka kya khayal hai?

**CLASS:** *(Placard 1)* "KHANDAAN MEIN NAAK KAT JAYEGI!"

**GOHAR:** Haan bhai, naak toh pehle hi kat gayi. Ab daal mein namak bhi kam hai. [UNFREEZE]

---

### SCENE 2 — BIRYANI (2 min)

**ABBU:** *(majboori mein)* Theek hai... biryani mangwa leta hoon. 15 degh.

**PHUPPO:** 20! Aur boti waali! Log plate mein boti dhoondte hain, dua nahi!

*(Faraz andar aata hai, seedha degh ki taraf.)*

**FARAZ:** Assalam o Alaikum. *(degh kholta hai)* ...Biryani kab aayegi?

**ZOYA:** Faraz! Dada ji ka janaza hai!

**FARAZ:** *(serious, sar hila ke)* Haan. Allah maghfirat kare. *(pause)* ...Boti waali hogi na?

*(Class hansegi.)*

**GOHAR:** [FREEZE] Special report! Pakistan mein janaze ab teen hisson mein hote hain: 10 percent dua, 20 percent rona, aur 70 percent... biryani review! Hamare reporter Faraz sahab live hain. Faraz sahab, aap ka review?

**FARAZ:** *(camera ki taraf)* Do star. Raita nahi tha. [UNFREEZE]

---

### SCENE 3 — DADI (2 min)

*(Phuppo Dadi ki taraf jati hai.)*

**PHUPPO:** Ammi, aap ne yeh kya pehna hai? Halka gulabi? Abba ko gaye 3 ghante hue hain! Log kya kahenge, widow aur rang?! Kal se sirf safed. Aur chooriyan utaar dein.

*(Dadi chup. Dheere dheere apni chooriyon ko dekhti hain.)*

**ZOYA:** Phuppo! Yeh chooriyan Dada ji ne pichli Eid pe di thin!

**PHUPPO:** Isi liye toh! Ab Abba nahi rahe, toh chooriyan kis ke liye?

**GOHAR:** Log, aap ka kya khayal hai?

**CLASS:** *(Placard 5)* "MOHALLA KYA KAHEGA?!"

*(Dadi pehli dafa sar uthati hain. Sab chup.)*

**DADI:** *(aahista, sukoon se)* Shamim... 50 saal main ne woh pehna jo tumhare Abba ki Ammi ne kaha. Phir woh jo mohalle ne kaha. Phir woh jo tumhare Abba ne kaha. *(chooriyon ko chhoo kar)* Yeh pehli cheez thi jo unhon ne kaha "jo tumhe acha lage, woh pehno."

*(Lamba khamosh pause. Phuppo ke paas jawab nahi.)*

**DADI:** Yeh nahi utrengi.

---

### SCENE 4 — JAIDAAD (1.5 min)

*(Phuppo foran mood badal leti hai, Abbu ko kone mein le jati hai.)*

**PHUPPO:** *(sargoshi)* Rashid... ghar ke kaghaz kahan hain?

**ABBU:** Baji! Abhi kafan bhi nahi...

**PHUPPO:** Main sirf poochh rahi hoon! Mujhe kuch nahi chahiye. *(pause)* ...bas upar wala portion. Aur dukaan.

**ZOYA:** *(beech mein aa kar)* Aur Dadi ka hissa? Aur betiyon ka haq? Phuppo, aap khud beti hain!

**PHUPPO:** *(foran)* Haan toh main apna haq hi toh maang rahi hoon! *(ruk ke, Zoya ko dekh ke)* ...lekin tum mat maangna. Larkiyan haq maangti achi nahi lagtin. Log kya kahenge.

**GOHAR:** [FREEZE] Exclusive! Aik hi saans mein haq maanga bhi gaya aur haq maangna mana bhi kar diya! Pakistan mein qanoon betiyon ko wirasat mein haq deta hai, lekin bohat si aurtein apna haq le hi nahi paatin, aur unhein rokne waalon mein aksar aurtein bhi hoti hain! [UNFREEZE]

---

### SCENE 5 — OUTSIDER (1 min)

*(Aleena notebook le kar aahista andar aati hai.)*

**ALEENA:** Excuse me... main aik research kar rahi hoon. Aap sab baar baar "log" ka zikr kar rahe hain. Woh log kahan hain? Maine bahar dekha, sirf chaar aadmi hain, aur woh bhi biryani ka pooch rahe hain.

*(Sab chup.)*

**ALEENA:** Toh aap kis ke liye yeh sab kar rahe hain?

*(Koi jawab nahi. Aleena kone mein baith jati hai.)*

---

### SCENE 6 — CHITTHI (2 min) [sab se zaroori scene, hansi band]

*(Zoya Dada ji ke takiye ke neeche se lifafa nikalti hai.)*

**ZOYA:** Dada ji ne... yeh likha tha. Mere naam.

*(Sab us ki taraf mur te hain. Gohar mic neeche rakh deta hai. Zoya parhti hai.)*

**ZOYA:** *"Zoya beta. Jab yeh chitthi milegi, ghar mein log honge, aur shor hoga ke kya pakana hai aur kya pehenna hai. Main ne zindagi bhar wohi kiya jo log chahte the. Apne Abba ke kehne pe science parhi, halanke mujhe shayari pasand thi. Phir naukri ki kyunke 'log kya kahenge'. Mujhe aik larki pasand thi, lekin shaadi wahan ki jahan log khush the. Allah ka shukr hai, tumhari Dadi ne mujhe us faisle pe kabhi pachtane nahi diya, lekin unhon ne bhi meri tarah apni zindagi logon ke hawale ki. Mere janaze pe koi tamasha na ho. Daal chawal pakana, aur sab ko barabar khilana. Apni Dadi ko woh pehenne dena jo unhein acha lage. Aur apni Phuppo se kehna... maine unhein bhi maaf kiya. Mujhe log aaj tak yaad nahi rakhte. Mujhe sirf woh lamhe yaad hain jo main ne apne liye jeeye. Woh bohat kam thay."*

*(Lamba khamosh pause. Phuppo aahista chashma utaarti hai, pehli dafa sach mein ro rahi hai.)*

**PHUPPO:** *(aahista)* Abba ne... mujhe kabhi bataya hi nahi.

**DADI:** Tum ne kabhi poochha hi nahi, Shamim. Tum sirf logon se poochti rahin.

---

### ENDING (1 min)

*(Gohar aakhri dafa khara hota hai, lekin is dafa anchor ki tarah nahi, aam insaan ki tarah.)*

**GOHAR:** *(class ki taraf, aahista)* Log... aap ka kya khayal hai?

**CLASS:** *(sab placards)* "AUR LOG? LOG KYA KAHENGE?!"

**ALEENA:** *(khari ho kar, center mein)* Meri research ka jawab yeh hai: log kuch din yaad rakhte hain, phir bhool jaate hain. Lekin jo zindagi aap ne un ke liye nahi jee... woh wapas nahi aati.

**SAB ACTORS:** *(aik saath)* **LOG TOH CHALE JAYENGE. ZINDAGI AAP KI THI.**

*(Sab sar jhukayein. Faraz aakhri lamhe mein degh se aik daana utha ke kha leta hai. Halki hansi ke saath light comic release. KHATAM.)*
